Project 05

OEM Digital Promotions/Offer Tracking & Analysis

Offer execution dashboard and SKU-level offer validation

Role

Project Manager (Team Lead)

Users

Global category managers

Ownership

End-to-end ownership from data crawl to front-end development, QC, and client-facing POC management

Business Impact

Annual revenue of $150K

This project tracks weekly updates of offers executed by OEMs with 29 retailers across NA, EMEA, LATAM, and Asia. Real-time screenshots are captured as proof of execution by crawling PDP and in-cart pages.

Promotions range from seasonal category-wide offers to one-day sale promos. Offer data is matched with extracted selling prices to validate rebates, quantify units sold under each offer, and evaluate ROI and promotion effectiveness.

Offer Execution Dashboard

SKU-level Offer Validation

Validate offer execution, rebates, and SKU-level linkage against tracked pricing and payouts.

Solution 02

E-commerce/Quick-commerce - Dynamic Digital Shelf Comp Set Analysis

Executive quick-commerce shelf intelligence across pricing, EDLP, banners, ratings, and assortment

Business Problem

Quick-commerce platforms change pricing, stock visibility, banner messaging, ratings signals, and assortment depth very frequently, making it difficult for teams to track who is winning the shelf in real time and where action is needed.

Business Impact

Built to help category, pricing, and digital commerce stakeholders quickly identify platform leadership, shelf risks, promotional pressure, and assortment gaps, enabling faster intervention and sharper channel decisions.

A consulting-led executive HTML dashboard concept designed to benchmark Blinkit, Zepto, Amazon Now, and BigBasket across digital shelf performance. The solution brings together price index, EDLP leadership, stock health, banner visibility, ratings and reviews, assortment breadth, and GenAI-led narrative insights into one decision-ready interface.

This solution was designed to simplify quick-commerce competitive benchmarking through a structured digital shelf view. It helps users compare platforms across executive KPIs, price movement, EDLP strength, banner messaging, stock consistency, shopper trust signals, and assortment depth. The addition of GenAI shelf insights turns the dashboard from a reporting layer into a decision-support tool by surfacing the most important commercial signals in simple business language.
